Bayside Sailing Club

Learn to sail on the bay, from your first tack to your first race.

About the Club

We have been teaching on the bay since 1974. Our instructors are certified through Sail Canada , and our fleet is maintained by volunteers.

Lessons

Lessons run from May to September. Beginners start in a two-person dinghy with an instructor on board.

  • Learn to Sail, ages 10 and up
  • Adult Beginner, weekday evenings
  • Racing Clinic, Saturday mornings
